Get the microphone

The browser gives the microphone only to a secure page. Typing works here.

  1. Open the secure address.
  2. On Android: if Chrome shows a warning, tap Advanced, then Proceed.
  3. On an iPhone: first open the lab's certificate in Safari and tap Allow. Open Settings, then General → VPN & Device Management, and install the profile. Then open General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on lab-os.
