Put the camera in front of you, facing you. Your right hand moves the gripper. Hold a left pinch (or Space) to move: the clutch. A right pinch closes the gripper. Let go and the arm holds still.

If your right hand leaves the picture, the arm stops and stays frozen. Let go of the clutch, then pinch again to continue. X = STOP. Esc = stop webcam control.
