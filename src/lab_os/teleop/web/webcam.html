How to drive

Put the camera in front of you, facing you. Your right hand moves the gripper. Tilt it to tilt the gripper, and turn it to turn the gripper. Hold a left pinch (or Space) to move: the clutch. A right pinch or a fist closes the gripper. It stays closed until you open your hand. Let go of the clutch and the arm holds still.

The arm moves less than your hand, and toward and away from the camera it moves the least. To go further, let go of the clutch, move your hand back, and pinch again.

If your right hand leaves the picture, the arm stops and holds still. Let go of the clutch, then pinch again to continue. X = STOP. ` (the key above Tab) = stop webcam control.
