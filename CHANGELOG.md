# Changelog

One line per meaningful change, newest first. Demo versions get a git tag (e.g. `demo-2026-11-15`).

## 2026-11-12
- Voice: OpenAI's voice is now `fable`, British and livelier (`style:` sent as instructions, `speed: 1.15` in `models.yaml`); a spoken answer's text appears as it's said, not seconds before.
- Voice fix: OpenAI's streamed WAV (header says "endless") made a spoken answer count as ~25 h in the spend log; the brain now rewrites the header and counts the real length.
- **The wake word** (step 7): "hey jarvis" heard on the brain itself (openWakeWord's models in `models/openwakeword/`, run with `onnxruntime`; nothing sent until the phrase), on screens that choose it (⚙ → This screen → Talking to it → wake word); a small dot in the status strip while it listens; after the phrase the core listens and the question ends after a silence. The persona's `wake_word:`, the venue's `wake_word:` (default: off on public venues). Several screens: the best one answers. A stuck or busy TTS model is skipped once (15 s timeout).
- Voice: `speak:` in a voice setup can list several TTS models; one over its limit (HTTP 429) rests until its retry time and the next one speaks. The Gemini setup lists four (the free key allows 3 requests a minute per model).
- **Dictation** and the per-screen **way in** (step 7): ⚙ → This screen → "Talking to it": push-to-talk (default) or dictation (tap the mic, the words go into the ask line while you talk, it stops after a short silence or a second tap; Enter sends, Esc or any key stops and keeps it). Greyed out with the reason where the screen can't listen. The greeting's hint follows the choice.
- **The spoken greeting** (step 7): after the start tap the greeting is also spoken, plus "You can talk to me: hold the mic." where the screen has a microphone. The designed hub asks for it at the tap and plays it while the greeting is typed after the jump. Silent with no voice, no internet or the screen's sound off.
- **Voice, push-to-talk** (focus step 7, `docs/voice.md`): a mic button next to "Ask anything" (hold it, or Space, and talk). The page streams the microphone to the brain (`/ws/voice`), the brain hears it while you talk (Gemini's `gemini-3.5-transcribe-live` on the free key), asks the same conversation as typing, and speaks the answer sentence by sentence (`gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`); the core follows `listening` / `thinking` / `speaking` and the real loudness. Voice setups in `config/models.yaml` → `voices:` (`main` OpenAI → `backup` Gemini), picked by the persona's `voice:` (both personas: `main`). No secure page, no microphone or no voice at the brain: no mic button. `/api/status` has `voice`; `in_use` has `"voice"` until the last spoken word; voice lines in the spend log.
- **The green arms in lab-os** (focus step 11): `so101-green` (follower) and `so101-green-leader` are `borrowed` devices, no longer off limits: `lab-os serve --arm so101-green` drives the follower like the white arm (lab venue), its calibration read only from LeRobot's cache (through a temporary copy); `so101_setup` refuses the greens; a port or camera another program has open (LeLab, a recording) is refused with a sentence; the two training cameras are listed by USB socket (the camera-model block is gone). The guard hook and `.claude/settings.json` allow the green ports and reading the calibration, and still deny writes there and changes to the `lerobot` env. The training launcher also refuses while another program has a green port open.
- The read tools include `docs/design/` (the design isn't a surprise any more).
