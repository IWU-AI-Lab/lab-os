# Quick jobs

Small jobs that are **already decided** and can be built in one Claude Code session without the lab, or nearly. A session told "do some quick jobs" takes them from the top, does each completely (its own branch, tests without hardware, docs, `FEATURES.md`, a PR), and moves it to "Done" with the date and PR number. Anything that needs the lab gets a line in `docs/guides/lab-checklist.md` instead.

Not decided yet? It goes in `docs/plan/ideas.md`, not here. Bigger work is a focus step (`docs/plan/current-focus.md`).

## Queue
Two coding sessions may run at the same time, one for each lane (the builder, 2026-11-20). The prompt for lane 1: "Read AGENTS.md, then docs/plan/quick-jobs.md. Do the jobs in Lane 1 from the top, one at a time, until the lane is empty. Do not stop to ask between jobs." For lane 2, the same with "Lane 2". Each job moves to "Done" with its date and pull request. Each job adds its lab steps: a step for a moment of the demo goes to `docs/guides/lab-checklist.md` (the demo run); every other step to `docs/guides/lab-checklist-after-demo.md` (its rules: at most 3 questions in one step). Until Tuesday 2026-12-01, the builder wants as many jobs done as possible; after that, only fixes.
