Wake Word
Verifier—

1. The Word

The wake word answers more often when it knows your voice. Sit where you sit to talk to it. Use the microphone that the hub uses.

Open this page on the brain's computer. The clips are for its microphone.

2. Say the Word

Press Record, then say the word once. Change the tone each time. Say only the word.

—

    3. Talk Without It

    Press Record 10 s, then talk as you talk to people: questions, numbers, names. Do not say the word. Room noise also helps.

    4. Train

    Train teaches the word your tries. Then the hub uses it at once, on every screen. A clear "the word" from another person still wakes it.
